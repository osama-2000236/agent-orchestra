# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith. Keep feedback on the work, not on the person.
Do not harass anyone, and do not publish private information about anyone.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling, sexualised content, and publishing someone
else's private data without consent.

Report a concern by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or by contacting the maintainer through
their GitHub profile at https://github.com/osama-2000236. The maintainer reviews reports and responds as soon as
practical. Reporters are not named without their consent.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code. Repeated or serious violations can lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

The full text and the enforcement guidelines are in the Contributor Covenant 2.1 link above.
